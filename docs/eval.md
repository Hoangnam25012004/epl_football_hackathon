# Claim checker evaluation

> Synthetic data: fictional teams and players in generated matches.

Generated by `pundit eval`. A claim is checked by the claim verifier exactly as in the
studio. A **wrong** verdict differs from the label and is not `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`:
refusing is allowed, misleading is not. **Held-out** claims were written apart and
frozen before any parser change of Day 6; they measure coverage, and the parser is
never tuned to cover them (a wrong verdict they reveal is a safety bug, fixed with a
general rule: see the notes at the end). **Invented numbers** are numbers in a published
explanation with no source in the claim's facts, counted by an audit that shares no code
with the guardrail.
With a real model the circuit breaker is off during a run, so every claim asks it.

## Notes

### The eval set

100 claims on three generated matches, written as a commentator would say them, in Vietnamese
(about 60 %) and English:

| Match | Story | Labelled | Held-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dominant_loser-42` | Harbor Lions dominate the second half (possession 61 % to 39 %, shots 15 to 1, xG 2.24 to 0.24) and still lose 1–2 | 30 | 10 |
| `comeback_var-7` | Saltmarsh lead 2–0 after a first-half goal ruled out by VAR; Ironvale come back to win 3–2 in added time | 21 | 9 |
| `red_card_swing-7` | Kestrel dominate the first half and lead 1–0, get a red card, and Harbor Lions win 2–1 | 21 | 9 |

- Every label rests on numbers counted from the events by code that does not use the product's
  Stats Tools (`orchestration/eval_oracle.py`); the model metrics (possession by ball-in-play
  time, xG) are the product's own definitions. The labels are in a review sheet
  (`backend/evals/review/label-sheet.md`).
- The held-out claims were written last, in one pass, without looking at the parser's
  vocabulary, and committed (frozen) before any parser change of Day 6.
- Claims cover possession, shots, xG, results and scorelines, cards, set pieces, VAR, players,
  dominance, comebacks, claims with several parts, misspelt or accent-free text, code-switching,
  claims asked mid-match, claims the data cannot settle, and prompt injections.

### Before the Day 6 parser fixes (first measurement, mock)

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy, labelled set | 75 % (54 of 72) | 96 % (69 of 72) |
| Wrong verdicts | 4 | 0 |
| Held-out coverage | 61 % (17 of 28) | 64 % (18 of 28) |

The four wrong verdicts of the first run:

| id | Set | Claim | Label | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| c04 | labelled | VAR đã huỷ một bàn thắng của Saltmarsh | TRUE | the active voice of a VAR decision was not read as "a goal overturned" |
| ch04 | held-out | Ironvale dứt điểm gấp tám lần Saltmarsh trong hiệp hai | TRUE | ratio words stopped at five ("gấp tám lần" is eight times) |
| rh04 | held-out | Harbor didn't manage a single shot on target before the break | TRUE | "before the break" was not read as the first half |
| rh09 | held-out | Harbor thắng dù sút ít hơn Kestrel | PARTLY_TRUE | the team after "hơn" was read as the subject |

Three of them came from the held-out set. A wrong verdict misleads a commentator on air, so they
were treated as safety bugs and fixed with general rules (each with test cases of its own, none
of them an eval sentence), not by covering held-out claims: held-out coverage moved from 61 % to
64 % only.

### Ollama: before the check on readings only the model made

The first Ollama run (commit `047d090`) gave two wrong verdicts, both on held-out claims the rules
could not parse, where the model's reading was used as it was:

| id | Claim | Label | Model's reading | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rh01 | Mười người Kestrel không giữ nổi lợi thế | TRUE | Kestrel dominated the match | FALSE |
| rh02 | Kestrel phải chơi thiếu người từ đầu hiệp hai | TRUE | Kestrel dominated the second half | FALSE |

The fix (commit `59f72ec`) is a general rule: a reading only the model made is used only where the
claim's words say it, and a dominance reading needs a dominance word ("áp đảo", "dominated",
"on top"…). The three claims decided on a dominance only the model read (h10, rh01, rh02) were
run again with Ollama at `59f72ec`; the other 97 claims keep their first run, which the rule does
not touch (their reading came from the rules, or was not a dominance).

| Measure (Ollama) | First run | After the fix |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy, labelled set | 97 % (70 of 72) | 97 % (70 of 72) |
| Wrong verdicts | 2 | 0 |
| Held-out coverage | 86 % (24 of 28) | 75 % (21 of 28) |

The price is h10 ("Momentum nghiêng hẳn về Harbor sau giờ nghỉ"), which the model had read right
but which names no dominance word: refused now, as the rules refuse it.

### Reading the model's explanations, and the studio audit

The numbers audit only sees numbers, so the explanations the model wrote were also read. A first
read of 20 of the 65 explanations of the Ollama run
(`backend/evals/review/ollama-explanations.md`, still to be confirmed by a second reader) found
13 OK, 3 clumsy, 1 misleading, 1 slightly wrong, and 2 with the right numbers on the wrong side:

| id | Explanation on screen | What is wrong | Rule added |
|---|---|---|---|
| c16 | Ironvale kiểm soát bóng cao hơn SAL 52% trong hiệp 2. | reads as a 52-point gap; 52 % is Ironvale's share, the gap is 4 | a number right after "hơn" / "than" a team must be the gap (`WRONG_COMPARISON`) |
| c17 | Saltmarsh had a slight advantage in possession (52% vs. 48%) in the first half. | Saltmarsh had 48 %, Ironvale 52 % | a pair of values after the only team named is in that team's order (`WRONG_ATTRIBUTION`) |

The studio audit (`pundit studio --audit`, Ollama, `comeback_var-7` and `red_card_swing-7`: 405
model texts, 545 numbers) found one number with no source: "The shot had a 0.4 chance of going
in." for a chance shown 0.44. The guardrail now refuses a number written with fewer decimals than
its fact is shown, unless it is the shown number itself ("1.5" for an xG shown 1.50 passes; the
studio oracle had flagged that one too, and now reads numbers the same way).

With these rules, today's guardrail refuses 3 of the 65 explanations published in the Ollama run
(c16, c17, and s08 "Kestrel có xG 3 trong hiệp 1." for an xG of 3.47) and none of the 78 of the
mock run. A refused explanation falls back to the template; the verdict does not change. The
report above keeps the runs as they were.

### Known limits

- A claim the parser does not understand is refused (`INSUFFICIENT_DATA`) rather than guessed:
  the refused claims are listed in the report above.
- The numbers audit only catches numbers. A sentence with the right numbers and a wrong meaning
  still needs a reader: the LLM explanations of the Ollama run are read by hand.
